Your Study Spark assessment

There are 15 study-habit questions and 40 Multiple Intelligences (MI) reflection statements. Every question is mandatory.

Part 1 · Student and subject details

Required fields and questions are marked with *. The website will stop submission if anything is unanswered. Your answers are processed in this browser; this demo does not email or upload your report.

Part 2 · Study habits and challenges (15 questions)

Question 1 selects your challenge pathway. Questions 2–15 will be generated for that challenge and your selected subject/curriculum.

Part 3 · Multiple Intelligences (MI) reflection

For each statement, choose one answer: 1 = Not at all like me · 2 = A little · 3 = Sometimes · 4 = Mostly · 5 = Very much like me.

Know Your Mind ✦ Unlock Your Potential

Welcome to Study Spark! We believe that every student has the potential to learn, grow, and discover study methods that work better for them. Our goal is to help students understand their study habits, recognise their challenges, and take small, meaningful steps towards more effective learning.

Please read the following terms carefully before using our platform.

1. Purpose of Study Spark

Study Spark is a student-led educational initiative designed to help students reflect on their study habits, learning preferences, and academic challenges. Based on the answers provided, the platform offers personalised suggestions and practical strategies that students can explore and gradually incorporate into their routines.

2. Gradual Improvement and Individual Progress

Study Spark aims to support students in developing more effective study habits over time. Consistently applying suitable strategies may help students improve their understanding, organisation, confidence, and approach to learning.

However, progress is individual. Results may vary depending on a student's needs, circumstances, consistency, and other factors. Improvement may take time, and no particular academic result or increase in marks is guaranteed. We encourage students to be patient with themselves and view learning as a continuous process.

3. Personalised Recommendations

The recommendations provided by Study Spark are based on the information students enter into the questionnaire. They are intended to serve as helpful starting points for exploring different study approaches.

Students are encouraged to try the suggested strategies, reflect on what works for them, and make reasonable adjustments. Not every recommendation will suit every student, and the suggestions should not be treated as fixed rules or the only way to learn.

4. Learning Preferences and MI Questionnaire

The Multiple Intelligences (MI) section is intended to encourage self-reflection about students' interests, preferences, and perceived strengths.

The results are exploratory and should not be interpreted as a definitive measure of intelligence, ability, or future potential. They are not a psychological assessment or diagnosis. No student should be labelled or limited by their results.

5. Educational Support, Not a Replacement for Guidance

Study Spark is an educational support tool. It does not replace teachers, qualified educational professionals, psychologists, or other appropriate specialists.

Students who continue to experience significant difficulties with learning, concentration, understanding, or academic stress are encouraged to seek guidance from a trusted teacher, parent, counsellor, or qualified professional where appropriate.

Seeking help is a positive step towards learning and growth.

6. Accuracy and Responsible Use

Students should answer the questionnaire honestly and carefully to receive recommendations that better reflect their experiences.

Study Spark endeavours to provide relevant and useful suggestions. However, the suitability and accuracy of recommendations may vary, and the platform does not guarantee that every suggestion will meet an individual student's needs.

The information provided should be used thoughtfully and alongside the student's own judgement and appropriate guidance.

7. Privacy and Personal Information

Students should provide only the information requested by the platform and avoid entering sensitive personal details into open-text fields.

In the current browser-based prototype, questionnaire responses are processed within the website to generate the report. Responses and reports are not automatically transmitted to a server or emailed by this prototype.

8. Use of the Platform

Students agree to use Study Spark respectfully and for its intended educational purpose. They should not attempt to disrupt the website, misuse its features, or misrepresent its recommendations as professional assessments.

Parents, guardians, and educators are encouraged to support students constructively, without using questionnaire results to compare, pressure, or label them.

9. Continuous Development

Study Spark is a developing student-led initiative. We may improve the platform, revise questions, or update recommendations as we learn from feedback and testing.

Although we aim to make the platform useful and accessible, uninterrupted availability and error-free operation cannot be guaranteed.

10. Acceptance of Terms

By proceeding with the Study Spark assessment, you confirm that you have read and understood these Terms & Conditions and agree to use the platform responsibly.

If you do not agree with these terms, please do not proceed with the assessment.

Our Promise to Students

Study Spark is not here to tell you that you are failing. It is here to help you discover what you can try differently.

Your current marks do not define your entire potential. With reflection, appropriate strategies, consistent effort, and support when needed, you can work towards developing study habits that serve you better.

Small steps matter. Progress takes time. Every student deserves the opportunity to grow.
